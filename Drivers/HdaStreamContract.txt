R4OS HDA stream contract
========================

Scope
-----
HDA.R4D owns conversion into the active hardware format, the software PCM
queue, the cyclic DMA ring, HDA interrupts and stream recovery. AUDSVC owns
application stream multiplexing; callers do not know the HDA period size.
The current hardware format is 48,000 Hz, stereo, signed 16-bit little endian.
The required stream descriptor and converter format is 0x0011: 48-kHz base,
16-bit field value 1 and stereo channels-minus-one value 1. Format 0x0021 is
20-bit and must never describe these S16 DMA buffers. Since 0.71.7 HDA.R4D
requires the codec PCM/stream capabilities and programs and verifies 0x0011
on the selected converter before assigning stream 1/channel 0. The
stream descriptor receives the same format after its reset.

Controller discovery and command transport
------------------------------------------
HDA 0.3.15 initializes up to four class-04/03 PCI functions as independent
controller instances. Each owns its command transport, codec graph, PCM
queue, DMA ring, interrupt context and work handles. Further controllers
produce a visible capacity warning. A digital controller with no connected
receiver remains enumerable when its converter and pin topology are usable;
it does not prevent an analog controller from initializing. Failed instances
quiesce their DMA and IRQ resources and restore their PCI Command word.
Output switches reuse these bounded instances and stop the previous PCM
stream; command transport and allocated ring storage remain available for
presence queries and later activation. PCI discovery, mappings and MSI
allocation occur during initialization, never during a user route switch.

The controller is always quiesced and passed through CRST low/hold/high,
including a firmware-initialized state. Command-DMA stop, both CRST edges and
post-reset STATESTS use monotone tick deadlines. Discovery evaluates all 15
codec addresses, every advertised audio function group and valid node ranges
through exclusive node 256. A failed command, malformed range, duplicate node
or capacity violation makes the candidate incomplete instead of truncating it.

CORB/RIRB is the normal transport. The driver negotiates 2-, 16- or 256-entry
rings, constrains DMA for 32-bit controllers, resets both hardware pointers,
handles wrap and full conditions and accepts only a matching solicited codec
response before the shared command deadline. Memory error, overrun, mismatch,
unsolicited response and timeout remain distinct observations. Immediate
Command is disabled by default and is attempted only after the explicit
`OPTION HDA immediate=on` opt-in; `OPTION HDA corb=off` may force that visible
diagnostic path. A normal QEMU boot must report `corb-rirb` and zero fallback.

Codec topology and analog route
-------------------------------
Every widget connection list is read in short or long form. Range entries are
expanded into their normative individual NID order, and that expanded index
is retained as the SET_CONNECT_SEL/amp-input index. First or consecutive
range markers, backwards ranges, NID zero, nodes outside the supported
eight-bit space and bounded edge-pool exhaustion invalidate the candidate.

The per-codec directed graph follows sink-to-source HDA connections. A bounded
cycle-safe breadth-first search accepts only present nodes in one audio
function group and a complete pin -> mixer/selector -> stereo output
converter route. Analog routes accept analog converters, and HDMI routes
accept digital converters. Missing nodes, cross-AFG edges, cycles without a
converter and incomplete routes fail closed. Output admission requires the
proven route and the advertised 48-kHz/S16 capabilities.

Pin Config Default port connectivity and device select only speaker,
headphone and line-out pins. SPDIF, HDMI and other digital devices never enter
the analog ranking. The deterministic order is speaker, headphone, line-out;
within a role a nonzero/lower association, lower sequence and lower NID win.
The converter and every route widget must advertise stereo. The resolved
widget/AFG capability set must contain PCM, 48 kHz and 16-bit support.

The bounded codec verb plan has one safe order:

- Set the audio function group and every power-controllable route widget to
  D0, then poll the reported actual state through a monotone deadline.
- Clear and verify the converter stream/channel, wait one tick, set and verify
  converter format 0x0011.
- Program the selected input on every selector and any ambiguous pin. Unmute
  the selected mixer/selector/pin input and every route output amp on both
  channels. Gain is the advertised 0-dB offset clamped to NumSteps; no fixed
  gain payload or broad codec quirk is permitted.
- Enable output and headphone drive as advertised, set EAPD only when the pin
  capability permits it, then assign and verify stream 1/channel 0.

The route and exact bounded verb-plan sequence are diagnostic output. A
presence-capable headphone pin is read at initialization and then at most once
per 250 ms through the existing status/write path under the stream lock.
Trigger Required is honored. Presence selects the proven headphone route;
absence selects speaker, then line-out. If the pin has no trustworthy sense
evidence, the same speaker/line-out/headphone fallback stays fixed and is
reported explicitly. Unsolicited jack responses remain disabled; any later
activation must use the same hardened IRQ/Worker path.

HDMI and output catalog (0.78.16)
--------------------------------
Each controller retains at most 32 physical output routes. Their identities
combine PCI bus kind/BDF, PCI vendor/device, codec vendor/device/address and
pin NID. They do not depend on enumeration order or the current monitor name.
A firmware change that relocates PCI devices can change this identity; the
service must then report and apply its normal fallback policy.

HDMI requires a digital output pin with a display capability, Presence Detect
and ELD Valid, a bounded ELD v2 baseline and an explicit stereo LPCM SAD for
48 kHz and 16 bits. Invalid/truncated data, absent receivers and unsupported
formats remain distinct states. DisplayPort is recognized but its packet
format is not enabled by this HDMI implementation. Names are sanitized from
the ELD monitor-name bytes. Queries and writes refresh digital presence at
most once per 250 ms. A lost active sink stops PCM and disables its pin and
digital converter; it is no longer reported as active.

The HDMI verb plan adds digital PCM enable, a two-channel converter, FL/FR
slot mapping with unused slots disabled, and the checksummed stereo Audio
InfoFrame. Packet transmission is enabled before assigning the stream.
Deactivate disables packet transmission and digital output and clears the
converter stream/pin. A failed route switch attempts to restore the previous
route and returns a failure instead of publishing a successful selection.

AudioBackend v3 consists of the unchanged 72-byte v2 prefix plus the generated
24-byte AudioOutputExtension. Kernel 0.1.104 accepts v2 unchanged and validates
the v3 size before reading callbacks. Audio callback context retains the R4D
owner across waits and completion operations. R4AUDIO v2 appends output-info
and stable-ID selection after all older slots; AUDSVC owns normal user choice.
Initial playback prefers an available HDMI sink, otherwise an available
analog/default output. Persistent preference and desktop controls belong to
0.78.17.

Hardware boundary: the NVIDIA display engine must also enable HDMI audio and
supply presence/ELD to HDA. Limine framebuffer output alone is not proof of
that setup. No arbitrary GPU register programming or modeset is performed by
HDA. On OssiPC after the 2026-09-08 update, both controllers initialized and
eight outputs were enumerated. NVIDIA 09:00.1 (10DE:228E, codec 10DE009F)
reported all four HDMI pins absent. Its graphics function 09:00.0 (10DE:2504)
has no native display driver; bootfb remains active. The display handoff and
HDMI stream evidence therefore remain open. No HDMI PCM was sent to an
unavailable receiver. An external NVIDIA display owner must first establish
the active connector/head and current receiver capabilities. The HDA packet
format follows Intel HDA 1.0a; NVIDIA's description of the display/audio split:
https://download.nvidia.com/XFree86/gpu-hdmi-audio-document/ (section 5).

Focused validation: nine codec/HDMI owner cases, five topology cases, generated
Zig/C contract conformance and one SMP4 guest with two HDA controllers passed.
Both guest routes produced eight backend writes with zero new backend errors;
an invalid output identity retained the preceding route, and AUDSVC Close
succeeded. These are stream/ownership checks, not acoustic HDMI evidence.
Evidence: Temp/roadmap-078-audio-execution/07816/ in the coordinating workspace.

Write contract
--------------
- The public backend descriptor admits 8,000 through 192,000 Hz, mono or
  stereo, and U8 or S16LE input. The kernel checks these copied descriptor
  limits before dispatching an R4D write; HDA repeats the check before taking
  the stream lock or entering queue and resampler work. Rejected input returns
  service_api_result_invalid without accepting frames.
- writePcm accepts non-empty, frame-aligned packets in a supported input
  format while the bounded playback reserve has room. At the 32-period target
  it returns service_api_result_busy without consuming data. Packet boundaries
  never become DMA boundaries.
- Streaming conversion preserves partial source frames between calls. A
  complete converted frame is appended exactly once to the software queue.
- The queue contains 64 hardware periods. Capacity failure is reported and
  counted; old audio is never overwritten.
- Ordinary writes do not add silence to reach a period boundary. Drain pads
  the last partial period once. An exhausted, still-open stream may also emit
  its remaining partial period once before parking. Both the first short
  block and a parked restart receive a 10-ms start deadline from the first
  pending write. Adjacent writes may combine without postponing that deadline.
  The kernel audio owner's delayed progress callback flushes a remaining
  partial period and permits a one-period start; no subsequent application
  write, close or status query is needed. No permanent idle polling is added. Drain appends three explicitly
  counted zero periods after all requested frames so codec and host-backend
  queues can emit their buffered tail before RUN is cleared; these postroll
  periods are not underrun recovery.

DMA geometry and ownership
--------------------------
- One period is 480 frames or 1,920 bytes, exactly 10 ms in the hardware
  format. The ring contains 64 128-byte-aligned periods in one contiguous DMA
  allocation; a separate BDLE describes each period.
- BDL, CBL and LVI are programmed while RUN is clear. All 64 BDLEs have IOC
  set and remain unchanged for the complete RUN phase.
- PeriodBook tracks producer, current hardware period, ready ownership and
  queued count independently. The producer may not write the active hardware
  period and may not overwrite a ready period.
- GCAP input, output and bidirectional counts are summed within the exact
  30-descriptor controller limit. A dedicated output descriptor is preferred;
  a bidirectional fallback has its output-direction bit set. Missing route,
  format capability or descriptor capacity fails before RUN.
- Playback starts with two ready periods (20 ms) and then admits PCM up to a
  32-period running reserve (320 ms). Drain or an expired pending-start deadline
  may start with one ready period. A validated DMA position entry and LPIB select the current
  cyclic period; implausible positions cannot address outside the fixed ring.
- A consumed period is cleared before reuse. Silence is therefore observable
  only when hardware reaches a period for which no PCM data was available.

Interrupt and refill contract
-----------------------------
- MSI is preferred. INTx is accepted only when PCI configuration supplies a
  representable line together with pin 1 through 4. Missing exact evidence is
  a visible failure; no neighboring GSI is inferred or registered.
- INTCTL enables the selected stream and global controller interrupt. The
  stream descriptor enables IOC, FIFOE and DESE reporting.
- The ISR reads INTSTS and SD_STS, counts BCIS/FIFOE/DESE exactly once per
  observation, clears only the reported write-one-to-clear bits and atomically
  latches both error classes for coalesced deadline work.
- The ISR allocates nothing, waits nowhere, calls no Completion API, copies no
  PCM and emits no normal log stream. Driver Work advances ownership from the
  plausible DMA/LPIB position, fills complete periods and performs a pending
  recovery.
- Every request declares a deadline 10 ms after submission, a stable HDA
  serialization key and a callback budget derived from 2 ms but never above
  the DriverApi-v20 four-tick maximum. It enters the separate bounded EDF
  lane and executes on the single `r4d-audio` short-completion worker, not the
  normal IRQ/task Driver FIFO.
- Every callback performs exactly one bounded refill or recovery pass. At most
  one successor is queued for HDA. Two completion handles permit it to coexist
  with the currently returning callback without unbounded allocation.
- A monotone IRQ generation closes the worker-completion race: work that
  arrived during a refill pass is represented by a freshly deadline-stamped
  successor or already belongs to the successor claimed by the racing IRQ.
  A pass that could not acquire the stream lock also retains a successor,
  even without a new IRQ generation. HDA keeps its error latch until the lock
  is acquired; AC97 likewise exchanges its pending status only inside the
  acquired owner. A contended progress/status callback reports Busy for a
  bounded follow-up. Completion inspection/release is serialized between worker and task context
  by an atomic gate. Shutdown claims the gate and must cancel or join every
  retained handle before DMA can be released.

Underrun and recovery
---------------------
The controller DMA position buffer is 128-byte aligned, sized to eight bytes
per real descriptor and constrained to the advertised address width. Enable,
base readback, entry offset, reset clearing, shutdown disable and free order
are bounded. DMA position and LPIB must both lie within CBL and agree within a
two-period read skew; unavailable, invalid or repeatedly divergent DMA data
degrades visibly to LPIB.

Since HDA 0.3.16, an underrun means a missing hardware period despite later
prepared PCM or at least one complete software period waiting for DMA. Each
such period increments underruns and silence_refills. An earlier gap remains
counted even if the same delayed observation eventually reaches an empty ring.
The backend contract carries no producer deadline: complete source exhaustion
cannot establish whether a pause was intended. It is recorded as an idle
transition instead of generating another error every 10 ms. Upstream drops,
backend errors and service latency stay separate counters. Repeated
observation of one value
does not refresh the last-movement deadline. Wrap is accepted as progress;
freeze, an elapsed-time-impossible jump, FIFOE and DESE request one controlled
recovery. Recovery accounts and clears queued software and ready-ring frames,
so stale audio cannot replay. The first underrun may emit one warning, but the
normal path remains silent. If hardware advances beyond the remembered
producer, the next producer slot is realigned immediately after the current
hardware period; newly accepted PCM is never written behind the consumer.

After source exhaustion, the already-cleared ring emits three more periods
of silence for the same codec/host tail protection used by Drain. Newly
prepared PCM cancels this pending idle transition. After the postroll, the
driver clears RUN and descriptor interrupts, resets the descriptor and ring
ownership, and retains the logical stream, software queue and resampler phase.
No periodic completion work continues on the parked ring. New full periods
restart at descriptor zero with the normal prefill. An already parked, empty
Close only resets the logical conversion state; it does not replay postroll.

Lifecycle
---------
Open and the first writes fill the queue and ring. An application stream Close
pumps remaining canonical PCM and requests the bounded backend Drain. HDA
emits queued requested frames and the three-period postroll before clearing
RUN. On a 400-ms drain timeout it performs a forced stop, accounts and clears
software PCM, ready periods and resampler state, and only then returns the
error. Driver shutdown uses the same bounded Drain and may continue safe
teardown after that forced stop. Both paths
use a monotonic RUN-clear timeout of at least 10 ms as required by Intel
HDA033-A. BDL/CBL/LVI are reprogrammed only while stopped, so a later stream
starts from a defined position. Stop, error, Close and shutdown share
idempotent cleanup and release pending Driver Work.

An explicit ledger mirrors concrete ownership in acquisition order: PCI,
MMIO, reset, transport, discovery, route, stream DMA, optional position DMA,
IRQ, backend and Driver Work. Partial composite acquisitions are still freed
from their actual handles. The owner tests inject failure after every ledger
stage, require reverse release and repeat the complete teardown without a
second release. A transport, IRQ/MSI or completion that cannot be proven
quiescent vetoes further unsafe frees and retains ownership for retry.

AUDSVC creates only a logical client stream at Open. HDA therefore receives
no open, DMA start or zero-filled PCM quantum until the first non-silent
write. A complete silent service block is consumed without a backend write;
if HDA is active, the service closes it once through this same bounded Drain.

BDL, stream and position allocations are constrained below 4 GiB whenever
GCAP does not advertise 64-bit addressing. Shutdown drains and stops RUN,
disables controller interrupts, unregisters IRQ routes, rolls MSI back and
joins Driver Work before disabling and freeing position, stream or BDL DMA and
before unregistering the audio backend.

Measurement surface
-------------------
The monotone progress tracker records observation and actual byte movement
separately. Re-reading an unchanged position cannot mask a freeze. Circular
wrap is computed within CBL; a movement larger than elapsed-time throughput
plus two periods is impossible. After one complete 640 ms ring window without
movement, or after an impossible position, HDA stops and resets the descriptor,
zeros the complete ring and drops software-queued and ready-ring stale PCM
before later playback can restart. Old contents are never replayed as current
audio.

The existing audio backend and performance status expose write/refill counts
and durations, ring fill, drops, underruns, silence periods and errors. A
status snapshot refreshes ownership from the plausible DMA/LPIB source before
publishing the fill level; this keeps producer pacing valid when QEMU coalesces
or delays IRQ work.
Two bounded `HDA.DIAG` records expose this state through LOGSVC. `core` names
the selected PCI function and ID, GCAP stream counts, command transport,
codec/AFG, analog pin-to-converter route and format. `run` names phase, IRQ
mode, DMA/LPIB position mode, write/underrun, FIFO/descriptor,
position/stream-recovery, timeout/drain, work-drop and live ownership values.
The immutable core record is refreshed once on the first runtime Close so it
cannot age out before the late Test-profile diagnostic. Close and shutdown
may emit aggregate records; writes, verbs and individual interrupts do not.
The bounded HDA.IDLE close record adds idle stops, resumes, partial tails,
IRQ/work submission totals, padding frames and explicitly dropped frames.
Its idle counts expose source exhaustion even when it is not a backend error.
Driver Work performance snapshot v2 adds deadline queue/running/high-water,
queue ticks, start misses, callback-budget overruns and admission rejections
globally and for the HDA owner.

The 64x10-ms contiguous DMA geometry remains unchanged in 0.69.42. Existing
cursor, queue, underrun and deadline observations did not demonstrate a cost
that justifies changing periods or adopting segment DMA; the roadmap permits
that change only after such evidence.

------------------------------------------------
On OssiPC with HDA 0.3.15, an open stream after its last PCM block generated
20 extra underruns, silence refills and deadline jobs in a controlled 200-ms
empty interval, with no hardware errors. HDA 0.3.16 passes eight ring/queue
owner cases and a short SMP4 playback/pause/restart/tail/Close probe. The same
guest idle interval produces zero jobs and zero underruns. Its continuous
200-ms refill interval, deadline checks and Close pass; the close log reports
three idle stops/resumes, two partial tails and zero dropped frames.

------------------------------------------------
HDA 0.3.11 passes its owner build and the complete 134-module workspace build.
The clean Test image passed both standard and four-CPU SMP headless acceptance
with HDA active, zero upstream drops, driver underruns, driver errors, backend
