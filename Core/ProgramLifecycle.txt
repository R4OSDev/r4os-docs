R4OS program lifecycle
======================

Identity and storage
--------------------

Programs, tasks and threads are stable heap objects addressed by an ID and a
generation. Registries use reusable tombstone slots and chunked storage;
removing one object does not move another live object or its locks.
Numeric stress counts are test baselines, never capacity declarations.

Lifecycle
---------

A program is admitted, receives its R4M0 image and payload ownership, resolves
imports, creates its root task and enters R4XStart. Normal exit, requested
close, kill and failure all converge on ordered cleanup. Handles remain
generation-checked so stale observers cannot operate on a reused slot.

Hard kill and reaping may continue only when the target holds no tracked lock
and has no unwind guard. Owner resources are detached before their backing
payload is freed. Program, task and thread registries are not locked across
I/O, wait, yield, callback or resource destruction.

Inventory
---------

R4SYS exposes paged, restartable program/task/thread inventory. A generation
change requests a restart and requires the caller to discard partial pages.
R4DEV exposes diagnostic summaries. Fixed-layout compatibility structures
are never extended in place; new telemetry uses append-only versions.
