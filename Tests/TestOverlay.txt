R4OS test image overlay
=======================

The Distribution Test profile overlays
`Repositories/Distribution/TestInjection/` on the normal `Injection/` tree.
It may add test-only configuration, fixtures and startup behavior without
changing the Slim or Full image.

Test-only modules use `IMAGE_SCOPE=test` or an explicit Test-profile include.
Production modules must not depend on files that exist only in the overlay.
The image planner rejects unknown scopes, duplicate targets and missing
artifacts.

`Tools\Build.bat -test` builds the Test workspace, creates and verifies the
image, starts QEMU headless and evaluates the guest markers. The complete
test inventory is maintained in `Docs/Inventory/Tests.json`.
